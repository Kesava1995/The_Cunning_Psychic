Synopsis

Kusha has spent years pretending to be ordinary.

Most people believe he is simply observant.

His family knows better.

Strange incidents seem to follow him wherever he goes. Problems are avoided before they occur. Accidents are anticipated. Questions are answered before they are asked.

Yet Kusha never explains anything.

While others dismiss the coincidences, one person begins noticing the pattern.

Prajwala.

Friend. Engineer. Observer.

And perhaps the first person willing to trust what she cannot explain.

Main Characters

Kusha

Mechanical engineer. Patient observer. A man who seems to know more than he should.

"He always knows more than he says."

Prajwala

Intelligent, observant, and quietly courageous. One of the first people to notice that Kusha's explanations never fully explain anything.

"She notices more than she lets on."

The Family

Behind Kusha stands a family that already knows there is something unusual about him.

  • A practical father.
  • A worried mother.
  • A curious elder sister.

Genre & Themes

Genres: Psychological Mystery, Supernatural Fiction, Slow-Burn Romance, Slice of Life

Themes: Trust, Friendship, Family, Patience, Knowledge, Responsibility, Consequences
