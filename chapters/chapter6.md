**Chapter 6: The Answer**

Exactly one month had passed since the chaotic night of the rescue, and
life had seemingly returned to its quiet, predictable rhythm, at least
on the surface. The local newspapers had eagerly moved on to newer,
fresher stories, the intensive police investigation continued entirely
without Kusha\'s involvement, and Rakesh\'s young son was finally back
home safely. The sprawling factory floor was just as overwhelmingly busy
as ever, yet for Kusha, certain profound things remained quietly
unresolved.

The expansive shopping mall was incredibly crowded for a typical
Saturday afternoon. Families moved in dense clusters between the
brightly lit stores, energetic children ran excitedly ahead of their
exhausted parents, and generic pop music drifted softly from speakers
hidden high in the ceiling. Near the vibrant display window of a popular
clothing store, Aparajita was carefully holding up two tiny, brightly
coloured shirts for her children.

\"Blue or yellow?\" she asked, turning to her husband.

Vijay examined both garments with an exaggerated, highly theatrical
seriousness before pointing confidently. \"Blue.\"

\"The last five things you selected were entirely blue,\" Aparajita
pointed out, her eyes narrowing in mock suspicion.

\"That is simply because blue is a vastly superior colour,\" Vijay
defended effortlessly.

Aparajita let out a dramatic groan and rolled her eyes. Kusha stood
patiently nearby, effortlessly carrying two heavy shopping bags.

\"You chose to marry him entirely voluntarily,\" Kusha reminded his
sister with a faint, amused smile.

\"It was the absolute biggest mistake of my entire life,\" Aparajita
declared dramatically.

\"See?\" Vijay gestured toward her triumphantly. \"She admits it openly
now.\"

Kusha let out a genuine, warm laugh as the three of them finally stepped
out of the crowded store. Their extensive shopping trip was very nearly
finished, requiring just a few more essential items before they could
finally leave.

That was the exact moment Prajwala unexpectedly entered the bustling
mall. She had only come to the shopping centre to quickly buy a birthday
gift for her cousin, expecting absolutely nothing more from the mundane
errand. She walked casually through the grand entrance, carrying her
handbag, until her eyes suddenly landed on him, causing her footsteps to
halt completely.

For a fleeting, disorienting moment, the overwhelming noise of the
crowded mall seemed to fade entirely into the background. There was
Kusha, standing barely thirty feet away from her. He was casually
talking to Aparajita and Vijay, laughing openly, and looking completely,
infuriatingly normal. He looked exactly as though he had not completely
overturned her entire foundational understanding of reality months
prior. He looked as though she had not spent the entirety of the past
month thinking about him obsessively, almost every single day.

Her racing heart immediately betrayed her practiced calm. Her first,
overwhelming instinct was that she should leave immediately. That was
undeniably the most sensible option: turn around, walk away, go back
home, and pretend this unexpected encounter had simply never happened.
Instead, rooted by her quiet courage, she remained standing right there,
completely unable to move.

Kusha noticed her, because, true to his nature, he always noticed
everything. Their eyes locked across the distance, and neither of them
looked away. Prajwala felt every single carefully arranged, logical
thought in her mind completely collapse. It had been months, and yet she
was still reacting to his presence exactly like this. She closed her
eyes briefly, took a slow, grounding breath, and then bravely started
walking directly toward him.

Aparajita, ever observant, noticed her approaching first. The exact
moment she recognized Prajwala, her eyebrows shot up in surprise. Vijay
naturally followed his wife\'s gaze and let out a soft, understanding
*Oh*.

Prajwala stopped nervously before the group.

\"Hello,\" she offered quietly.

Kusha offered a gentle, reassuring smile. \"Hello.\"

She quickly turned her attention to Aparajita. \"How are you?\"

\"I am good,\" Aparajita replied smoothly.

Prajwala then nodded politely to Vijay. \"Hello.\"

\"Hello,\" he returned easily.

A few agonizing seconds of heavy silence immediately followed, the
specific kind of profound quiet that feels substantially longer than it
actually is. While Kusha and Prajwala awkwardly exchanged polite,
mundane questions, Vijay leaned slightly toward his wife. Keeping his
voice incredibly low, he asked, \"Is this the exact same girl?\"

Aparajita glanced carefully at him, then briefly at Prajwala, before
offering a single, silent nod of confirmation. Vijay\'s eyes widened
significantly as he let out another quiet *Ah*. Suddenly, absolutely
everything made perfect sense to him: the sudden panic, the abrupt
disappearing act, and the mysterious, lingering silence that had
followed. He immediately decided not to interfere, recognizing that this
highly emotional situation was clearly far above his pay grade.

Meanwhile, Prajwala was trying incredibly hard to appear perfectly
normal, but the fragile facade was clearly not working. Kusha noticed
her intense internal struggle immediately. Her verbal answers were
unusually short, her breathing was slightly erratic and uneven, and her
nervous eyes kept actively avoiding his gaze, only to repeatedly flutter
back to him before looking away once again.

Utilizing his signature patience, he waited calmly for nearly a full
minute. Finally, he asked softly, \"Are you okay?\"

That simple, gentle question completely broke something profound inside
her, primarily because she was absolutely not okay. She had not been
truly okay for months, not since that devastating day of realization.
Not after realizing with terrifying clarity that her lingering
suspicions regarding his abilities had been entirely correct, and
certainly not after realizing that she still cared for him deeply
anyway. She opened her mouth to speak, but absolutely no words managed
to come out.

Instead, driven by sheer emotion, she reached forward and grabbed his
hand incredibly tightly, holding on as though she was deeply afraid he
might simply disappear into the crowd. Respecting their privacy,
Aparajita quickly looked away, while Vijay immediately found the mall\'s
ceiling architecture highly fascinating; neither of them said a single
word.

Kusha looked thoughtfully at her trembling hand holding his, and then
shifted his deep gaze up to her face. He completely understood her
silent turmoil; in fact, he had understood it for a very long time,
perhaps far longer than she even realized.

\"Prajwala,\" he murmured softly, prompting her to slowly look up at
him. \"You absolutely cannot keep hiding forever,\" he told her gently.

Her fingers tightened slightly around his warm hand. For years, she had
meticulously hidden her romantic feelings, then she had carefully hidden
her growing suspicions, then she had hidden her profound fear, and
finally, she had hidden from him entirely. Every single time life had
demanded a difficult answer from her, she had consistently chosen
silence. Kusha had patiently allowed it to happen, until this exact
moment.

He spoke with absolute, gentle clarity: \"Do you still like me?\"

The sheer directness of the question completely shocked her, leaving
absolutely no avenue for escape, no room for ambiguity, and no place
left to hide---only the raw truth remained. Prajwala felt her face grow
incredibly warm. For a fleeting second, she seriously considered denying
it, but then she remembered exactly who she was talking to, realizing
that lying to him would be entirely pointless. Slowly, and with quiet
bravery, she nodded her confirmation.

Kusha smiled warmly, his expression radiating neither surprise nor
triumph, but simply a profound relief. It felt exactly as though a vital
conversation that had been delayed for years had finally, properly
begun. He then asked another, far more significant question: \"Do you
want to stay with me long term?\"

In that suspended moment, the bustling mall completely disappeared, the
surrounding people vanished, and the ambient sounds faded into absolute
nothingness. Only his monumental question remained, presenting the
ultimate, real choice. The choice was no longer about whether he
possessed psychic abilities, whether he held impossible knowledge, or
whether she fully understood him, because she didn\'t, and perhaps she
never truly would.

The actual, fundamental question was significantly simpler: Did she
trust him? She vividly remembered their years in college, the countless
small, meaningful moments, his unwavering kindness, his infinite
patience, and his comforting calmness. She weighed the strange
incidents, the impossible knowledge, the hidden secrets, and the
lingering fear, and realized that beneath absolutely all of it, he was
still the exact same person. He was still the same Kusha.

Her eyes became visibly moist as she nodded affirmatively once again,
and this time, she absolutely did not look away. For a long moment,
neither of them spoke, primarily because neither of them needed to; the
definitive answer had already been given. Kusha placed one arm gently
around her waist and smoothly pulled her closer. Prajwala\'s face
immediately flushed a deep crimson.

\"Okay,\" Vijay announced suddenly and loudly. \"We are definitely
leaving now.\"

Absolutely nobody had asked them to leave, but nobody needed to, as
Aparajita was already actively walking away. \"Take your time,\"
Aparajita called over her shoulder.

\"That is very generous of you,\" Kusha called back smoothly.

\"You are quite welcome,\" Vijay replied. \"Just don\'t make it weird.\"

The two of them quickly disappeared toward the mall\'s main exit. Left
entirely alone in their own private bubble, Prajwala finally let out a
laugh---a small, completely relaxed laugh, her very first one since she
had initially spotted him. Kusha looked deeply at her, and she looked
right back. There was absolutely no more fear, no more running away, and
no more unanswered questions demanding immediate, logical explanations;
there was only pure, unconditional trust.

Slowly, she stepped forward and securely wrapped her arms around him.
Kusha warmly embraced her back, holding her safely close while the dense
crowd of shoppers flowed obliviously around them, entirely unaware that
something incredibly important had just occurred.

For the very first time since she had fled from his house months earlier
in sheer panic, Prajwala felt completely at peace. It was not because
she magically understood absolutely everything about his reality, but
because she had finally, bravely accepted that she simply didn\'t need
to.

And for the very first time in a remarkably long while, the infinitely
patient Kusha finally stopped waiting. Because the profound answer he
had confidently known for years had finally, beautifully been spoken,
entirely without words.
