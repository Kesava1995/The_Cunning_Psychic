**Chapter 5: The Midnight Parcel**

The young boy let out a low, agonizing groan, the weak sound barely
managing to pierce through the steady, monotonous hum of the car\'s
engine. Kusha briefly shifted his sharp gaze to the rearview mirror,
carefully studying the fragile figure slumped in the backseat. The
child, who could not have been more than ten years old, was covered in
thick layers of industrial dust, his clothes heavily stained from days
of terrifying neglect. His face was deathly pale, and one of his thin
wrists bore a stark, angry red mark, clearly indicating that something
rough had been tightly bound around it until very recently. However, his
chest was rising and falling in a steady rhythm; he was undeniably
alive, and for Kusha, that was the absolute only thing that mattered.

Slumped heavily beside the boy sat a large, bearded man, completely
unconscious, with his thick neck tilted at a highly unnatural angle
against the cool glass of the window. His breathing was heavy and
measured, and a dark, ugly bruise had already begun forming prominently
near his temple. Even while completely incapacitated and dead to the
world, the man radiated a distinct aura of violence and danger.

Kusha calmly returned his unwavering focus to the empty road ahead. The
midnight hour had long since passed, leaving the typically bustling
streets of Pune remarkably desolate and quiet. Only the occasional,
distant rumble of a heavy transport truck disturbed the profound
silence, while the rhythmic passing of yellow streetlights cast
alternating flashes of harsh light and deep darkness across the
vehicle\'s windshield.

Suddenly, a harsh vibration shattered the quiet. It was not Kusha\'s
personal phone, but rather the heavy, battered device belonging to the
unconscious bearded man in the back. The cracked screen illuminated
brightly in the dark cabin, displaying an incoming call. Utilizing his
signature patience, Kusha deliberately let the device ring twice before
finally swiping to answer.

\"Boss?\" a gruff, highly impatient voice demanded through the speaker.

Kusha flawlessly adjusted his vocal cords, dropping his tone to
perfectly mimic the rough, authoritative rasp of the older gang leader.
\"The parcel is absolutely ready,\" he stated smoothly.

An audible sigh of instant relief crackled over the line. \"It is about
time.\"

\"The exact pickup location will be shared with you shortly,\" Kusha
continued, maintaining the commanding facade.

\"When?\" the man pressed anxiously.

\"In exactly twenty minutes,\" Kusha replied.

A harsh laugh echoed from the other end. \"Make it quick, the customer
has been waiting and complaining since the early evening.\"

Without offering a farewell, Kusha abruptly ended the connection. Over
the next several minutes, he methodically fielded another call from a
different number, projecting the same authoritative voice to issue
another set of strict instructions, and then he seamlessly handled
another, and another. In total, he managed six separate, incoming calls.
He expertly coordinated six highly distinct pickups, directing the
desperate buyers to six completely separate, carefully selected
locations scattered across the city.

By the time he abruptly ended the final call, the young boy in the
backseat began to stir weakly. His heavy eyelids fluttered open
slightly, revealing a deep, heartbreaking mixture of utter confusion and
profound fear. For a fleeting, disorienting second, his frightened gaze
locked directly onto Kusha\'s eyes in the rearview mirror, but sheer
physical exhaustion rapidly overpowered him, pulling the child back
under a dark veil of sleep. The heavy phone vibrated yet again, this
time buzzing with a demanding text message requesting a precise
location. Operating with cold, calculated efficiency, Kusha swiftly
replied by dropping a digital pin. He then sent another precise
location, and then another, perfectly distributing six unique, isolated
addresses located primarily near the desolate outskirts of Katraj.

**The Trap Springs**

Miles away, deeply hidden within a sprawling, abandoned warehouse
compound near Katraj, a sleek white SUV slowly rolled to a confident
halt. Three tough-looking men stepped out into the humid night air. One
of the men casually adjusted his shirt, revealing a heavy, loaded pistol
securely tucked into his waistband, while another methodically
chain-smoked, his eyes darting around the shadows. The third man,
visibly younger than the rest, appeared highly nervous, shifting his
weight anxiously from foot to foot. Standing alone in the dim moonlight
beside a parked van was a solitary supplier, an impossibly large,
overstuffed duffel bag resting heavily near his boots.

\"You are incredibly late,\" the supplier grunted, his voice laced with
clear irritation.

The smoker simply shrugged indifferently, taking another long drag.
\"Traffic,\" he offered, the single word dripping with careless apathy.

Looking thoroughly unimpressed by the weak excuse, the supplier gestured
to the ground. The smoker crouched low, his hands moving quickly as he
unzipped the thick canvas bag. Inside, several neatly arranged, tightly
wrapped rectangular packets lay in perfect, organized rows. Reaching in,
he pulled one of the heavy packets out, examining it closely under the
moonlight. He pressed the tight plastic firmly between his fingers to
test the density before using his thumb to violently tear open a small
corner. A highly refined, pristine white powder immediately became
visible, catching the faint light.

The nervous younger man leaned in closer, his eyes widening. \"It looks
completely alright to me,\" he whispered.

The smoker stood up slowly, keeping his eyes firmly fixed on the waiting
supplier. \"The boss explicitly guaranteed that the quality would be
exactly the same as the last shipment. Is it?\"

The supplier spat a thick glob of saliva onto the dirt floor. \"Do you
honestly believe I would still be standing here waiting for you if it
wasn\'t?\"

A rough, appreciative laugh escaped the smoker. Acknowledging the fair
point, he gestured for the others, and the heavy bag of illicit
narcotics was swiftly loaded into the back of the waiting SUV. Within
mere minutes, the transaction was finalized, and the vehicle sped
aggressively out of the compound.

However, exactly ten minutes later, their dark, silent journey was
abruptly and violently interrupted. A blinding explosion of flashing red
and blue emergency lights suddenly ignited the darkness directly behind
them. The driver spat out a vicious string of desperate curses,
instantly slamming his foot down on the accelerator. The smoker
panicked, his hand frantically reaching toward the loaded weapon tucked
snugly in his waistband. But it was already entirely too late. Heavily
armed police vehicles flawlessly executed a tactical maneuver,
aggressively boxing the SUV in from absolutely every conceivable
direction. Heavy metal doors flew open, and highly trained officers
swarmed forward with their weapons drawn and leveled. The swift, highly
coordinated arrests took less than two minutes to execute completely.

Simultaneously, scattered across the dark city, five other identical
illicit pickups occurred flawlessly, with five other criminal groups
leaving their respective locations feeling completely satisfied.
Consequently, five more criminal groups were violently intercepted and
formally arrested long before the sun even had a chance to rise.

**Deliveries**

The very first breaking news alerts began flooding mobile screens
shortly after the gentle glow of dawn broke the horizon. The bold
headlines screamed of a massive victory: *Major Drug Network Completely
Busted*. Subsequent lines detailed the impressive scale of the
operation: *Multiple Arrests Executed Across Pune*, followed closely by
*Unprecedented Quantity of Illicit Narcotics Safely Seized*. The final
line of the alert simply stated that the authorities were actively
withholding further, sensitive details regarding the anonymous tip that
led to the bust.

Sitting quietly in his vehicle, Kusha read the glowing notification
before calmly folding the gang leader\'s phone entirely shut. The final,
deceptive call had been perfectly completed. The final, illicit pickup
had happened exactly as scheduled. The final, crucial arrest had been
successfully made. There was only one vital task remaining on his list.

He smoothly turned the steering wheel, leaving the main highway behind
as he navigated the car onto a distinctly narrow, unpaved rural road.
The sprawling, concrete skyline of the city slowly faded away behind
him, replaced by isolated stretches of open land. In the back seat, the
young boy remained locked in an exhausted, unconscious state. Beside
him, the large, bearded gang leader remained equally unconscious,
entirely unaware of the catastrophic collapse of his empire. Neither of
the passengers had any earthly idea where they were currently heading.
Eventually, a pair of bright, waiting headlights pierced the darkness
ahead. A single, solitary vehicle was parked off the side of the road,
its engine idling quietly as it waited.

Kusha brought his car to a smooth, controlled stop directly parallel to
the waiting vehicle. For several agonizingly long moments, the profound
silence of the remote location remained completely unbroken, and
absolutely nothing happened. Then, with a loud, metallic creak, the
driver\'s door of the opposing car slowly opened. A man stumbled out
into the cool air, his physical appearance radiating a profound,
desperate exhaustion. He looked entirely broken, carrying the heavy,
frantic demeanor of a father who had not allowed himself a single second
of sleep in days.

The exact moment his weary eyes spotted the small, sleeping figure of
the boy through the rear window, he froze completely. His entire body
went rigidly still as shock washed over him. Then, abandoning all
hesitation, he sprinted desperately toward the car. He frantically
wrenched the rear door open, his hands visibly trembling as he reached
inside. For several, heart-wrenching seconds, he simply stood there and
stared, as though he was deeply, fundamentally afraid that if he
blinked, the beautiful mirage of his child might cruelly vanish back
into the night. Then, letting out a shattered sob, he pulled the boy
tightly into his trembling arms. Hot, uncontrollable tears spilled
instantly down his cheeks. The sudden movement caused the young boy to
slowly open his heavy eyes, his weak voice calling out, \"Dad\...\"

The older man\'s face completely crumpled under the overwhelming weight
of the moment. Pure relief, staggering disbelief, and infinite gratitude
crashed into him all at once. Respecting the intense intimacy of the
reunion, Kusha quietly looked away, staring straight ahead into the
darkness. He firmly believed that some profoundly emotional moments
rightfully belonged exclusively to other people, not to him.

After several minutes of emotional embrace, the father carefully closed
the rear door and slowly approached Kusha\'s open window. Neither man
spoke immediately, allowing the heavy, silent gratitude to hang in the
air between them. Finally, the father managed to force a single, heavily
choked sentence past his lips: \"Thank you.\"

Kusha offered a simple, understanding nod. \"Please, take him home
immediately,\" he advised softly.

The man looked deeply at Kusha, his eyes betraying the fact that he
desperately wanted to ask a hundred impossible questions about how this
miracle had occurred. However, sensing the absolute finality in Kusha\'s
calm demeanor, he wisely asked none. He carefully helped his exhausted
child into his own vehicle, started the engine with a roar, and drove
swiftly away, the red taillights eventually disappearing entirely into
the consuming darkness.

Kusha patiently watched the road until the glowing lights vanished
completely. Once he was entirely alone, he stepped out of his vehicle
and opened the rear door. The large, bearded man was still slumped over,
completely unconscious. \"It is finally your turn,\" Kusha stated
flatly. The man naturally did not hear him, and even if he could, it
would have made absolutely no difference to his impending fate.
Utilizing his mechanical leverage, Kusha forcefully pulled the heavy man
out of the rear seat. The gang leader was significantly heavier than his
frame suggested, possessing the notoriously difficult, unyielding mass
of dead weight. After a brief, calculated physical struggle, Kusha
successfully dragged the man to the front of the vehicle and dumped him
unceremoniously behind the steering wheel. He reached across the massive
chest, pulled the seatbelt securely across him, and let it click firmly
into place.

Reaching down, Kusha confidently started the engine and expertly shifted
the transmission directly into first gear. The car immediately lurched
and began rolling forward, moving incredibly slowly at first before
catching a steady, relentless, idling momentum. Several kilometers
straight down that specific, isolated road, a highly alert team of
heavily armed police officers was already positioned and eagerly
waiting. Kusha took a large step back, moving safely off the edge of the
pavement, and quietly watched the ghost vehicle slowly carry the fallen
kingpin directly into the waiting hands of justice. Without waiting to
hear the inevitable sirens, he smoothly turned around and began the
long, silent walk away from the scene.

**The Foundations of a Rescue**

When the sun finally rose high over the sprawling industrial complex of
Lokhandi Dhatu, the entire factory floor was absolutely buzzing with
frantic, excited gossip. Dense clusters of workers huddled closely
together, their faces illuminated by the bright screens of their mobile
phones. Heavy machine operators entirely ignored their loud equipment,
enthusiastically debating wild theories, while strict floor supervisors
exchanged hushed, unbelievable rumors near the administrative offices.
The massive, unprecedented drug bust completely dominated every single
conversation occurring within the facility.

\"They found absolute kilos of hard narcotics,\" one worker claimed
wildly.

\"I heard they hit multiple, secret locations all at once,\" another
added confidently.

\"It was a highly classified Crime Branch operation,\" a third chimed
in, leaning closer. \"Extremely big, powerful people are definitely
involved.\"

Someone loudly claimed that a corrupt local MLA was going to be publicly
arrested next, while someone else firmly insisted that the massive drug
shipment had been imported directly from another state. The fundamental
truth was that absolutely nobody in the factory knew any actual facts.
Naturally, their complete lack of factual information did absolutely
nothing to stop them from talking endlessly. Standing near the edge of
the chaos, Kusha quietly sipped a steaming cup of tea. He listened to
the wild theories, occasionally offering a polite nod of agreement,
occasionally allowing a faint, mysterious smile to grace his lips, but
mostly, he remained entirely, comfortably silent---exactly the way he
always did.

However, the true roots of the morning\'s spectacular chaos had actually
been planted the previous day. Around the peak heat of noon, Kusha had
been walking past the main administrative corridor when he noticed his
floor supervisor, Rakesh, slowly leaving his private office. The usually
composed man looked substantially worse than his usual, highly stressed
self. While his professional clothes were perfectly neat and pressed,
his physical face was an absolute disaster. Heavy, bruised dark circles
sat prominently beneath his bloodshot eyes. His shoulders drooped
significantly, making him look exactly as though he were physically
carrying a crushing, invisible weight on his back. Kusha quietly
finished his tea, waited patiently until Rakesh had returned to his
desk, and then walked over, knocking lightly on the thick glass door.

\"Come in,\" a tired voice answered.

As Kusha entered the room, Rakesh attempted to force a polite,
professional smile. The hollow gesture barely lasted a fraction of a
second before completely collapsing.

\"Is absolutely everything alright, sir?\" Kusha asked, perfectly
controlling his tone.

The direct, highly personal question clearly surprised the older man.
\"Why do you ask?\"

\"Because you seem incredibly worried,\" Kusha observed calmly.

Rakesh let out a heavy sigh, leaning back deep into his leather chair.
For a fleeting moment, his face hardened, and it looked as though he
might defensively dismiss the inquiry entirely. Then, a profound shift
occurred within him. Perhaps it was the sheer, mind-numbing exhaustion,
perhaps it was the absolute terror consuming him, or perhaps it was
simply the desperate, overwhelming human need to finally confess his
burden to someone else. \"My son is missing,\" he whispered, the
devastating words barely audible.

Kusha remained perfectly still, offering a respectful, unbroken silence.

Rakesh swallowed hard, continuing his painful confession. \"It has been
three agonizing days,\" he admitted, his voice cracking violently under
the emotional strain. \"He has been completely missing for three entire
days.\"

The small office instantly became incredibly, unnervingly still. Outside
the glass, the massive industrial machines continued their relentless
hum, but inside the room, neither man moved a single muscle.

\"I received a terrifying call,\" Rakesh continued, his hollow eyes
staring blankly at the polished surface of his table. \"They
aggressively demanded one full crore.\"

Kusha simply listened, allowing the man to empty his soul.

\"If I dared to inform the police\...\" Rakesh stopped, swallowing a
thick knot of pure terror. \"\...they explicitly promised that I would
absolutely never see him alive again.\"

For the very first time since Kusha had joined the large company, his
fiercely independent, highly capable supervisor looked utterly,
completely helpless. He was not exhibiting his usual workplace anger, he
possessed zero authority, and he held absolutely no confidence. He was
simply a terrified, helpless father. \"I honestly didn\'t know what on
earth to do,\" Rakesh confessed, the admission seemingly costing him a
massive amount of his remaining dignity.

Kusha sat quietly for a long moment, deliberately allowing the heavy
silence to settle and provide a strange sense of comfort. Finally, he
stood up.

Rakesh looked up, confused by the sudden movement. \"Where exactly are
you going?\"

\"I am going home,\" Kusha replied smoothly.

A bitter, highly humorless laugh escaped Rakesh\'s lips. \"Well, that
must be incredibly nice for you.\"

Kusha offered a very small, understanding nod, completely unfazed by the
bitter sarcasm. He walked toward the door, pausing just before turning
the handle. \"What is your son\'s name?\" he asked quietly.

\"Karan,\" the broken father replied.

Kusha nodded exactly once, committing the name and the man\'s profound
pain to his memory, and then he left the office without another word.

**The Mechanism of Retribution**

Exactly one hour later, deep within the bustling heart of the city,
Vikram was aggressively working his way through a greasy plate of hot
samosas when Kusha suddenly arrived at the small restaurant.

\"You are incredibly late,\" Vikram noted, his mouth partially full.

Kusha ignored the complaint and slid into the creaky chair directly
opposite his friend. Vikram generously pushed a spare, empty plate
across the table, offering to share, but Kusha completely ignored the
gesture. Frowning deeply at the highly unusual behavior, Vikram put his
food down. \"You have an incredibly serious face on right now,\" he
observed cautiously.

\"Karan,\" Kusha stated flatly.

\"What?\"

\"He is ten years old,\" Kusha elaborated softly.

Vikram\'s casual, relaxed expression vanished entirely, instantly
replaced by a hard, professional intensity. All traces of their usual
joking evaporated. \"Are you talking about a missing child?\" he asked.

Kusha nodded silently.

Vikram let out a harsh, vicious curse entirely under his breath. For
several tense seconds, he simply stared at the stained surface of the
table, his mind rapidly processing the dark implications. Then, leaning
uncomfortably forward over the table, he lowered his voice to a
dangerous whisper. \"There have been a lot of highly disturbing reports
lately,\" he began cautiously.

Kusha waited patiently, knowing his friend had extensive connections
within the city\'s darker corners.

\"They are absolutely not official police reports,\" Vikram clarified
quickly. \"They are dark, underground rumors.\"

\"What specific kind of rumors?\" Kusha prompted.

Vikram lowered his voice even further, leaning closer. \"A massive,
highly organized trafficking network,\" he revealed grimly. All around
them, ordinary customers continued loudly eating and chatting,
completely, blissfully unaware of the horrific reality being discussed
inches away.

\"Are they taking children?\" Kusha asked.

Vikram nodded slowly. \"Mostly desperate runaways,\" he answered, though
his tone was heavy. \"Mostly.\"

The distinct emphasis on the word did not go unnoticed by Kusha\'s sharp
intellect, and his dark eyes narrowed fractionally.

Vikram continued, painting the bleak picture. \"Some of these innocent
kids are violently kidnapped off the streets,\" he explained. \"Some are
cruelly sold by desperate families.\" He sighed heavily. \"Some are
rapidly transported deep across state lines to avoid detection.\" His
jaw tightened visibly in anger. \"And lately\...\" he paused, struggling
to voice the darkest part. \"\...as if human trafficking wasn\'t
inherently bad enough\...\"

\"What else?\" Kusha pressed.

\"That exact same criminal network has recently started aggressively
moving hard narcotics,\" Vikram revealed.

Kusha remained entirely silent, absorbing the massive influx of crucial
information.

Vikram expanded on the terrible logic of the situation. \"The hidden
transportation routes already existed,\" he explained logically. \"The
heavily modified vehicles already existed.\" \"So, they simply expanded
their horrific business model.\" His expression grew exceptionally dark.
\"It means significantly more money for them.\" \"It means substantially
more risk.\" \"And it means infinitely more violence.\" He shook his
head in absolute frustration. \"And absolutely nobody in the police
department has managed to reach the heavily protected people running
it.\"

A heavy, oppressive silence fell directly over their small table. Vikram
let out another long, defeated sigh. \"The absolute worst part is that
we do not even possess a single clue regarding where the abducted
children are currently being held,\" he admitted bitterly.

Kusha slowly turned his head, looking thoughtfully out the greasy
window. A loud motorcycle rapidly passed by outside, its shape quickly
disappearing around a sharp corner. Returning his gaze to his friend,
Kusha spoke with an eerie, unshakeable certainty. \"I do.\"

Vikram blinked rapidly, entirely caught off guard. \"What did you just
say?\"

\"I know exactly where they are currently keeping him,\" Kusha stated
simply.

Utter silence enveloped them. The clattering plates and loud
conversations of the restaurant suddenly seemed to fade entirely into
the distant background. Vikram stared at him, bewildered. \"How on earth
could you possibly know that?\"

Kusha did not offer an answer, mostly because he absolutely never did.
The impossible question lingered heavily in the air for several seconds
before Vikram finally surrendered, aggressively shaking his head.
\"Forget it,\" he muttered, already entirely aware that he would never
pry a logical explanation out of his mysterious friend. \"Can you
physically take me to this exact location?\"

\"Yes,\" Kusha confirmed.

\"When exactly?\"

\"Right now,\" Kusha replied calmly.

Vikram stared deeply into Kusha\'s calm eyes, desperately trying to
determine whether the younger man was making a highly inappropriate
joke. He instantly realized he was not. The monumental realization
slowly, heavily dawned on Vikram. If Kusha\'s impossible intuition was
correct\... an innocent child might miraculously be rescued tonight. And
perhaps, if they played their cards perfectly, they could achieve much
more than just a single rescue. Vikram decisively pushed the plate of
unfinished, cold samosas completely away.

\"Let\'s go immediately,\" he declared, standing up. The small
restaurant bill remained entirely unpaid on the table, but given the
massive stakes of the impending night, neither man cared in the
slightest.

By the exact time Vikram managed to forcefully kick-start his loud
motorcycle, Kusha had already smoothly climbed onto the rear passenger
seat. \"Which specific direction are we heading?\" Vikram yelled over
the roaring engine.

Kusha firmly pointed his finger. \"Head straight East,\" he commanded.

Vikram aggressively twisted the throttle, accelerating rapidly into the
night. Approximately forty tense minutes later, they had successfully
left the bright, comforting lights of the sprawling city entirely behind
them. The wide paved highway rapidly narrowed into a treacherous road,
and the comforting streetlights became incredibly scarce before
vanishing completely. The towering industrial buildings gradually gave
way to desolate, empty plots of dirt and wild scrubland. Eventually,
Kusha reached forward and firmly tapped Vikram\'s shoulder.

\"Stop right here,\" Kusha instructed.

The heavy motorcycle screeched, rolling to a controlled halt in the
thick dirt. Directly ahead of them, looming aggressively in the
darkness, stood a massive, completely abandoned factory. Thick layers of
ugly brown rust covered the heavy iron gates entirely. The countless
broken, jagged windows reflected the pale moonlight like shattered
teeth. The entire property looked entirely dead and forgotten, which
made it the absolute perfect, secluded location for a violent criminal
enterprise.

Vikram studied the menacing structure closely. \"Are you absolutely sure
about this?\" he asked.

\"Yes,\" Kusha confirmed with quiet authority.

\"How many men are we currently dealing with inside?\" Vikram pressed.

Kusha closed his eyes briefly, allowing his claircognizance to reach out
into the silent space. The accurate answer arrived with the exact same,
undeniable certainty as the rising of the sun. He saw three distinct
individuals. One man was sitting inside the dusty main office. One man
was positioned guarding the dark rear entrance. And one heavily built
man was staying close to the frightened boy. \"There are exactly three
men,\" Kusha stated.

Vikram exhaled a long, steady breath of pure relief. \"Three is
completely manageable,\" he assessed confidently. He smoothly reached
deep into his heavy leather jacket, his hand emerging moments later
holding a solid steel object.

Kusha immediately raised a skeptical eyebrow. \"Do you routinely carry a
heavy wrench around with you in your jacket?\" he asked.

Vikram shrugged unapologetically. \"Professional mechanics are
incredibly dangerous people,\" he quipped darkly. For the very first
time that entire, highly stressful evening, Kusha offered a genuine,
faint smile.

They moved silently toward the structure. The heavy rear entrance of the
facility stood partially propped open. The first guard was lazily
sitting on a cheap plastic chair, his head nodding as he fought sleep,
while his other hand loosely gripped a glowing mobile phone. The
oblivious man absolutely never saw Vikram approaching from the deep
shadows. The heavy steel wrench connected violently and accurately with
the back of the guard\'s skull. The man collapsed instantly, dropping
like a stone. Vikram deftly caught the heavy body before it could hit
the hard concrete ground and make a sound.

\"Try to do it gently,\" Kusha whispered sarcastically, completely
ignoring his friend\'s violent methods as he moved deeper inside.

The vast interior of the factory smelled heavily of ancient, undisturbed
dust, but beneath that, there were other, far more unpleasant odors. The
sharp scent of old, degraded industrial oil mixed terribly with the
smell of damp, rotting concrete. And beneath all of that, there was the
unmistakable, metallic scent of pure, human fear. Faint voices echoed
strangely from somewhere far ahead in the dark maze. Kusha confidently
led the way. He possessed absolutely no hesitation, and his steps showed
zero uncertainty. Every dark turn, every hidden obstacle, and every
structural layout already existed perfectly mapped within his
extraordinary mind. They moved swiftly through a narrow corridor,
ascended a rusted metal staircase, bypassed a massive storage room, and
finally approached the main office. The vital information simply arrived
in Kusha\'s mind whole, requiring absolutely no logical explanation or
conscious effort. By this point in their friendship, Vikram had long
since stopped asking impossible questions.

The second hostile man was sitting comfortably inside the main office,
deeply engrossed in watching loud videos on his mobile phone. Because of
his distraction, his situational reaction time was incredibly poor. By
the exact moment he registered the intrusion and attempted to stand up,
Kusha was already standing directly beside him. Kusha delivered a
single, lightning-fast, highly precise strike directly to the side of
the man\'s exposed neck. The guard instantly collapsed heavily across
the wooden desk, completely unconscious. Knowing his own strength, Kusha
quickly checked the man\'s pulse to ensure he was not dead. The guard
was still breathing steadily. That was good; it meant only one violent
target remained. The gang leader.

Suddenly, a faint, heartbreaking cry reached their ears. It was not
particularly loud, nor was it perfectly clear, but it was unmistakably
the sound of a terrified child.

Vikram froze, his grip tightening on the heavy wrench. \"So, he really
is here,\" he breathed.

Kusha nodded affirmatively. The tragic sound originated directly from
the expansive main warehouse floor, hidden securely behind a massive
stack of rotting wooden crates. The two men moved incredibly quietly,
ensuring every single step was perfectly measured and every minute sound
was tightly controlled.

Then, peering around the crates, they finally saw him. The bearded man.
He was incredibly large, boasting a massive, broad-shouldered frame that
radiated physical menace. He was seated lazily on an old chair, with a
heavy, loaded pistol resting prominently on a nearby table directly
beside him. Several meters away, huddled pathetically on the cold, hard
floor, sat a deeply frightened young boy. The child\'s thin wrists were
tightly bound with rough rope. His eyes were violently red from hours of
endless crying. It was Karan.

Suddenly, the bearded man looked up. He was highly experienced in
violence and had vaguely sensed a subtle shift in movement within the
dark room. However, he had merely sensed movement, not immediate, lethal
danger, making him entirely too slow. His large, calloused hand
instinctively moved toward the resting pistol. Kusha, propelled by his
terrifyingly calm strategic mind, was substantially faster. A heavy,
solid metal rod happened to be leaning casually against a nearby defunct
machine. Grabbing it smoothly, Kusha delivered one massive, devastating
swing. The thick steel rod connected violently with the side of the
massive man\'s head, the sickening impact echoing loudly through the
cavernous warehouse. The heavy pistol fell harmlessly to the ground. The
massive man immediately followed it, knocked completely unconscious
before his heavy body even forcefully hit the concrete floor.

Profound silence instantly returned to the massive room. For several,
incredibly tense seconds, absolutely nobody moved. Then, completely
overwhelmed, Karan started crying openly. It was not a loud, piercing
scream, but rather a soft, broken weeping---the specific, devastating
kind of crying that only arrives after a victim realizes that hope has
miraculously returned.

Vikram quickly rushed forward, crouching gently beside the terrified
boy. \"It is absolutely okay now,\" Vikram promised softly.

The boy simply stared at the large stranger, completely terrified and
highly confused. Pulling a small knife, Vikram carefully cut the rough
ropes binding the child\'s wrists. \"You are Karan, right?\" Vikram
asked gently.

The boy offered a hesitant, trembling nod.

\"Good,\" Vikram smiled warmly, attempting to project safety. \"Your
father has been worried absolutely sick about you.\" Hearing his father
mentioned, the brave boy finally broke down completely, bursting into
heavy tears.

Several long minutes later, Karan was sitting safely wrapped in
Vikram\'s oversized leather jacket, greedily drinking from a plastic
bottle of water. He was still visibly shaken, his small hands trembling
slightly, but he was undeniably safe. For the very first time that
chaotic night, the dangerous mission could have easily ended right
there. A kidnapped child had been miraculously rescued, and a vicious
gang leader had been physically captured. Everyone gets to go home.

Instead, Vikram turned his head, looking thoughtfully toward the
massive, unconscious man bleeding slightly on the floor. His eyes
specifically drifted toward the glowing screen of the mobile phone
sticking out prominently from the man\'s pocket. A highly strategic
thought suddenly occurred to him. Given their deep connection, the exact
same, highly strategic thought occurred to Kusha at exactly the same
moment.

\"Exactly how many desperate people currently work under this specific
guy?\" Vikram asked, his voice hardening.

\"Many,\" Kusha answered simply.

\"Are there enough loyal members to completely disappear into the
shadows if this guy simply gets arrested immediately?\" Vikram pressed.

\"Yes,\" Kusha confirmed.

Vikram let out another vicious curse. He deeply understood exactly what
that grim reality meant. They would successfully rescue one innocent
child, but they would completely lose the expansive criminal network
forever. Moving quickly, Kusha bent down and effortlessly removed the
